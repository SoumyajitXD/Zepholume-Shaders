## Night-water investigation

[Night-water investigation](../artifacts/phase9/night-water-investigation.md) traces base environmental lighting, celestial terms, normal/view dependence, routing and alpha. The ordinary source is multiplied by the sampled lightmap, but reflected analytical sky and sun/moon highlights are subsequently added without local skylight visibility. `zephLightCoord.y` is available at the fragment, but not passed into `zephWaterSurfaceLinear`. Later material/scene shaping is not geometric reflection occlusion. Hand material routing does not accidentally send translucent held items into terrain water.

This is a **confirmed source limitation**. It is not proof that the supplied night image is defective or that its blue appearance comes predominantly from moon highlights. Normal-dependent artistic Fresnel can intentionally brighten open water at grazing angles. Alpha/depth/sorting remain runtime confounders. Nether and End celestial exclusions remain unchanged.

A cheap local-skylight attenuation is technically feasible, but the sky lightmap is ambient propagation rather than a directional sun/moon visibility test. A naive multiplier can darken overhang edges and open water incorrectly; total sampled irradiance would also let torches enable sky reflections. New shadow maps, tracing, rendering passes and arbitrary night multipliers were rejected as unjustified scope. No production change was made without the open/covered/cave/torch experiment. A demonstrated major lighting defect will block release; the present unverified concern is not automatically labeled a runtime failure.
