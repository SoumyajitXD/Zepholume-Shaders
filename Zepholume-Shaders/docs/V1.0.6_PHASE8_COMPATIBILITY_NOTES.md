## Remaining visual concerns

| Concern | Outcome | Source evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Excessive daylight contrast | **UNVERIFIED** shader attribution; no retuning | All five parsed profiles select exposure option 1 (multiplier 1.0), contrast option 1 (1.02), and saturation option 1 (0.98). There is no unusually high Ultra-only exposure/contrast setting. High/Ultra soften the face-light curve and add a small multiplicative hemisphere tint. Material shaping is bounded 0.95–1.04. Snow/foliage texture and source face shading remain confounders without matched controls. |
| Night water too bright | **UNRESOLVED** concern; cause in supplied image unproven | The base texture is lightmapped before `zephWaterSurfaceLinear`, but its reflected sky and celestial highlights are added afterward with no local skylight/occlusion argument. In an analytical Ultra fixture with zero base illumination, normal=view=sun, and full daylight, the sun lobe alone contributes working RGB `(0.828, 0.7776, 0.6624)` before later scene operations. The corresponding full-night aligned moon lobe contributes `(0.165, 0.204, 0.264)`. These are source-equation counterexamples to complete local-light suppression, not measured Minecraft pixel values or a diagnosis of the night capture. Do not select a global water brightness multiplier from them. |
| Additional cave lighting | **UNVERIFIED** defect | Ordinary lit terrain/entity paths multiply by the lightmap, suppress directional celestial attenuation as skylight falls from 0.65 toward 0.04, and apply multiplicative block-light warmth. Zero input remains zero through scene lighting/material/grade before fog. No additive global cave fill exists in that route. Fog and the water-specific additive terms are separate. The supplied cave observations establish no additional specific defect. |

The contrast expression was checked against the current source and sampled at seven nonnegative values for each supported contrast multiplier. All positive samples retained a positive toe. Minimum scale over those samples was 0.9672 for 0.96, 0.9964 for 1.02, and 0.9856 for 1.08. This small arithmetic check excludes the former affine negative-toe mechanism; it cannot judge artistic contrast or prove that final framebuffer highlights never clip.

## Architecture and compatibility contracts

- Fresh inventory found 28 root stages and 28 corresponding stages in each of `world0`, `world-1`, and `world1` (112 total). Each wrapper has the GLSL 330 compatibility declaration; Nether/End stages have their expected dimension macro. Existing `validate.ps1` additionally checks exact root/wrapper synchronization and linked interfaces.
- No shader source declares an extension or loader/version conditional branch based on `MC_VERSION`, `IS_IRIS`, or `OCULUS`. Shared compatibility inputs and existing uniforms remain requirements. A mocked Iris or conservative Oculus macro pass does not run those loaders or their transformers.
- The three vanilla dimensions keep separate wrapper macros. Overworld analytical sky and celestial water highlights are excluded in Nether/End; dimension color and ambient shape branches remain. Custom/modded dimensions need loader verification.
- Profiles are capability-clamped through `profile.glsl`; Potato removes analytical water/face/material work, Low has no vertex wave displacement because the wave function requires tier at least 2, and Balanced/High/Ultra enable their declared tiers. Required lightmap sampling is independent of optional face-light quality. No new extension, buffer, pass, or heavy feature is proposed here.
- `block.10001=minecraft:water` and the water-program material comparison separate actual water from other translucent terrain. The ID fits the documented older signed-short and current Iris ranges. Unknown IDs remain non-water. There is no support evidence for substituting a numeric vanilla block ID.
- `gbuffers_hand_water` is absent and inherits `gbuffers_hand`; it must inherit the repaired lightmap route. `gbuffers_water` is explicit in all dimension sets and otherwise falls back to terrain. Hand items must not inherit water material displacement merely because the item's texture is translucent.

Fresh focused validation: `scripts/tests/profile-config-tests.ps1` passed the five-profile/default/range/screen/label/tooltip/quality and malformed-input checks. The wrapper/version/extension inventory and profile grade assertions also passed. Full compilation, portability, source-derived GPU, packaging, and preservation checks are recorded centrally to avoid reusing historical pass counts as new results.
