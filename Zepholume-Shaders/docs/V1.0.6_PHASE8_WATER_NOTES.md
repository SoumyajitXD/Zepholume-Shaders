# Phase 8 water geometry interpolation investigation

2026-10-28. **PARTIALLY FIXED:** a triangle-dependent shader calculation is reproduced and corrected in source, numerical tests and native Intel GPU draws. The Phase 7 waterfall symptom remains **UNVERIFIED after the change**: no matched Minecraft baseline/treatment scene establishes that this calculation explains every photographed triangle.

## Located defect and correction

`shaders/lib/vertex.glsl` normalized the view ray at each vertex, then interpolated those unit directions. Normalization is nonlinear: for corners at unequal camera distances, the interpolated direction differs from the actual fragment-to-eye ray and depends on the chosen triangle diagonal. Water Fresnel and celestial half-vector calculations expose the error. The shader also interpolated vertex distances, introducing the same problem into fog, water transmission and material distance terms.

The vertex now transports the unnormalized `-viewPosition.xyz`. Existing water and world-elevation consumers normalize at the fragment evaluation point. The scalar distance varying and vertex length calculation are removed; `shaders/lib/fragment.glsl` derives `float zephDistance = length(zephViewDirection);` locally. These are geometry-interpolation corrections, with no artistic coefficient, profile value, texture coordinate, normal, wave, material ID, alpha or blend change.

Consumer audit: `zephWaterSurfaceLinear` normalizes its input; `zephWorldElevation` normalizes its input; `zephFogColour` currently does not consume the supplied direction; `zephMaterialResponse` currently does not consume its direction parameter. Their distance consumers now receive the fragment's geometric distance.

## Path audit and hypotheses

Root and all three vanilla-dimension `gbuffers_water` pairs exist explicitly; these routes do not need a fallback. The water vertex route includes profile, common and water libraries. `mc_Entity.x` within 0.5 of 10001 is classified as water, and `block.properties` maps that ID only to `minecraft:water`. Only classified water moves; other translucent terrain follows ordinary scene shading. Texture and lightmap coordinates retain their respective texture matrices. Normals retain the normal matrix and safe fragment normalization. The final alpha remains the clamped source texture/vertex alpha; there is no new discard, depth write or blend override.

The wave displacement depends on X/Z/time and changes Y only. A block-side vertical face at constant X or Z remains in that plane after displacement; therefore displacement alone does not establish nonplanarity of those waterfall faces. No wave restriction or arbitrary amplitude reduction was applied. The native reproduction succeeds with waves enabled in Balanced through Ultra, and the corrected diagonal difference disappears while those waves remain enabled.

This rejects the need to disable waves to fix the reproduced interpolation defect. It does not rule out separate chunk-phase seams, texture animation, two-sided normals, real fluid geometry, translucent ordering or loader-specific issues. Screenshot attribution and those hypotheses require a controlled Minecraft scene. Non-Overworld water still uses its existing reflected-sky branch and skips celestial specular. Cave/night reflection terms still have no local skylight-occlusion input; that limitation was not retuned here.

## Focused evidence

`scripts/tests/water-interpolation-tests.py` uses independent perspective-correct barycentric interpolation over 450 planar cases, including offsets, depths, slopes, sample positions and both diagonals. There are 375 old-ray diagonal counterexamples. Old maximum ray error is 0.7059923; old alternate-diagonal Fresnel delta is 0.2597530. Corrected maximum ray error is 5.55e-16 and diagonal ray delta is 5.83e-16. Old vertex-length interpolation has maximum distance error 0.9713775 and alternate-diagonal delta 0.2351603 view units; corrected maxima are 1.78e-15 and 8.88e-16. Explicit old-ray and old-distance negative controls both exit nonzero. JSON evidence is under `artifacts/phase8/water-interpolation-*.json` and `water-distance-negative.json`.

`scripts/tests/native-gl-render.py` adds opt-in off-center planar geometry, perspective projection and explicit triangle diagonals; prior fixture defaults remain. `scripts/tests/water-interpolation-gpu.py` performs 52 source-bound RGBA32F draws: baseline/treatment and both diagonals for 13 fixtures. **13/13 pass** on the available Intel native context. It covers all five profiles, Nether/End, non-water translucent material, underwater, a centered control and short-fog water/non-water/underwater cases. The baseline is a preserved shader copy from before the interpolation edits; final treatment variants are source-manifest checked.
