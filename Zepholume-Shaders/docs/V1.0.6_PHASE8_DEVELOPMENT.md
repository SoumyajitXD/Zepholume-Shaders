## Water investigation and correction (A)

The effective water programs exist explicitly at root and in `world0`, `world-1`, `world1`. Their shared vertex identifies `mc_Entity.x` near pack-local ID 10001; `block.properties` assigns only `minecraft:water`. The shared fragment keeps other translucent terrain on the ordinary scene route. Texture/lightmap matrices, normal transforms, alpha, depth and fallback behavior were traced. The pack has no custom water blend override, fragment depth write or new discard.

The defect is nonlinear data interpolation. The old vertex normalized `-viewPosition.xyz` before interpolation and separately interpolated `length(viewPosition.xyz)`. Unit rays and lengths are not affine functions of position. Unequal-distance corners therefore give a wrong per-fragment ray/distance and a result that depends on a quad's triangle diagonal. Water Fresnel and half-vector terms amplify the ray error; fog, transmission and material distance terms consume the distance error.

`shaders/lib/vertex.glsl` now transports raw `-viewPosition.xyz` and removes the distance varying and vertex length. `shaders/lib/fragment.glsl` calculates `float zephDistance = length(zephViewDirection);` at the fragment. Existing water and world-elevation helpers already safely normalize their input; the fog endpoint ignores its direction argument, and the material helper currently does not use its direction argument. No other consumer relies on unit-length transport. The actual fog equations and artistic coefficients are preserved.

Independent perspective-correct barycentric tests cover **450 planar cases**, including different slopes/depths/offsets and both diagonals. There are **375 old-ray diagonal counterexamples**. Old maximum ray error is 0.7059923; old diagonal Fresnel difference is 0.2597530. Corrected ray error is at most 5.55e-16. Old interpolated-distance error reaches 0.9713775 view units and diagonal difference 0.2351603; corrected errors are at most 1.78e-15 and 8.88e-16. Deliberately restoring either old calculation fails its negative control.

**13 native fixtures / 52 RGBA32F draws pass.** Baseline/treatment and both diagonals cover five profiles, Nether/End, water/non-water, underwater, centered and short-fog cases. The largest baseline diagonal RGB difference is **0.0694267**; corrected differences are at most **1.1920929e-7** (tolerance 3e-5). Compared alpha differences are zero and output is finite. Potato and distant-fog non-water controls match exactly. Short-fog non-water diagonal error falls from 0.0288270 to zero. Source manifests and evaluated shader hashes bind draws to their named inputs.

The original wave hypothesis was not accepted as a diagnosis. Existing waves move Y using X/Z/time: vertical block-side faces remain in their constant-X/Z plane. The native defect reproduces with waves enabled in Balanced–Ultra and is repaired without disabling them. No wave amplitude, orientation gate, normal or artistic water change was applied. Actual still/flowing fluid meshes, edges, chunk seams, texture animation, two-sided surfaces, overlapping transparency and sorting remain runtime questions. The fixture compares common face interiors, excluding wave-moved raster edges, and disables blending/depth: it does not validate Minecraft transparency composition.

Detailed evidence and per-fixture results: [water notes](V1.0.6_PHASE8_WATER_NOTES.md), `artifacts/phase8/water-gpu/report.json` and raw float pixels. This demonstrates a located GLSL defect, not the complete historical screenshot causal chain.

## Hand investigation and correction (B)

Every hand vertex already transports `gl_MultiTexCoord1` through `gl_TextureMatrix[1]`, exactly as the shared terrain/entity route. The fragment samples/applies `lightmap` only with `ZEPH_LIGHTMAP_LIT_PROGRAM`. The hand wrappers defined `ZEPH_HAND_PROGRAM` but omitted that flag; subsequent `zephGradeHand` bypasses scene lighting. This is the actual missing irradiance route, not a reason to reduce global hand brightness.

One flag is added before the shared fragment include in each of:

- `shaders/gbuffers_hand.fsh`
- `shaders/world0/gbuffers_hand.fsh`
- `shaders/world-1/gbuffers_hand.fsh`
- `shaders/world1/gbuffers_hand.fsh`

There is no explicit `gbuffers_hand_water`; the documented fallback is `gbuffers_hand`, so translucent hands inherit the correction without entering the terrain-water material route. Existing grade, alpha, no-fog policy and coordinate transformation remain. No new emissive-item or dynamic world-illumination feature was added.

The hash-verified frozen Phase 7 hand produces RGB **0.6509804** at synthetic irradiance **0, 0.25 and 1**, with identical raw pixels: it does not respond. The corrected hand produces **0, 0.1633833 and 0.6509804** across **all five profiles × three dimensions**. Unit-irradiance output and alpha are unchanged. There are **63 corrected draws plus three baseline draws**, including 18 immersed-media/alpha checks; all pass, remain finite and preserve alpha. Four source routes and eight missing/late-flag negative controls pass.

Evidence: `artifacts/phase8/hand/report.json`, individual fixture JSON and raw RGBA32F pixels. A 1×1 lightmap isolates irradiance multiplication; it does not verify spatial block-light versus sky-light sampling or a real hand/item. Empty hands, opaque/translucent held items, emissive blocks, nearby-light transitions, daylight appearance and underwater/dimension gameplay are still **UNVERIFIED**.
