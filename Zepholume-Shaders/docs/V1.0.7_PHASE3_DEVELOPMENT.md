## Development and review

Three concurrent subagents owned atmosphere, water/materials, and performance; the lead owned integration, CI, validation and packaging. Atmosphere implemented the sky change and CPU/native fixtures, then updated the camera-space regression. Water implemented bounded specular input and native water and lighting fixtures; it found no supported directional covered-water signal or reason to retune brightness. Performance added cloud and scene/hand specialization, specialization assertions and corrected wrapper define ordering. The lead added the CPU sky regression to CI and reviewed the integrations.

* Sky vertex output now passes affine view position. Default perspective-correct interpolation reconstructs the point on a planar primitive; the Overworld analytic fragment normalizes it and computes world-up elevation locally. Interpolating previously normalized corner directions and separately calculated elevation is nonlinear. No claim is made that real game sky geometry reproduces the synthetic artifact. Textured and non-Overworld paths avoid the new calculation.
* `zephSpecularLobe` clamps its input to [0,1] before the power chain, bounding roundoff amplification. A deliberately overstated native stress input gave 0.690006912 versus 0.690409362 with an in-memory clamp-removal mutation; the analytical bound was 0.6900069. This does not prove a visible game defect.
* Cloud normal elevation is removed from preprocessed disabled tiers; scene normal transport and calculation are removed where there is no consumer, including hand paths. Enabled non-hand consumer expressions are retained. Drivers may already eliminate dead work. Sky correction adds fragment work and water adds a clamp; no game performance gain is claimed.
* Lighting boundary draws and mutation checks were added without arbitrary brightness changes. The known absence of directional local sky visibility remains: neither ambient skylight nor block light reliably separates open sky, roofs and torch-lit caves. Non-water routing and alpha remain on their existing paths.

The Phase 2 source-model counterexample and a new 15-case perspective suite cover synthetic planar quads. Maximum old diagonal angle was 2.572727 degrees; corrected direction delta was 5.55e-17 and reconstruction error 7.28e-12. One deliberately restored vertex-normalization mutation was rejected. In 16 Intel UHD 730 standalone sky draws, maximum unequal-quad RGBA diagonal difference changed from 0.0292971 to 1.19e-7; the rotated case changed from 0.0292029 to 1.29e-5. Equal-radius and near-horizon cases also passed. Comparisons cover mutually drawn interior pixels, and do not establish actual loader geometry.
