## Native render method and evidence

`scripts/tests/native-gl-render.py` extends the Phase 3 ctypes WGL harness. It verifies the source manifest and every selected evaluated-variant hash, demands the installed Intel compatibility context, compiles and links the selected original source-derived vertex/fragment pair, and draws a full-screen quad. It binds deterministic 1×1 RGBA8 albedo and lightmap textures, supplies texture coordinates, normal, vertex colour, material ID, and documented synthetic loader uniforms. It renders to a 32×32 `RGBA32F` framebuffer with blending, depth testing, culling, dithering, and framebuffer sRGB disabled, then reads `RGBA` float pixels with `glReadPixels`. Each successful run stores `result.json`, `pixels.rgba32f`, and `render.png`. The result records driver identity, variant hashes and original source names, profile, dimension, texture values, uniforms, framebuffer format, center pixel, channel range, finite-value check, and OpenGL error. The clear colour differs from fixture output; a deliberate `--skip-draw` negative test exits with `changed=False`, proving the check rejects a framebuffer that was merely created and cleared.

The installed driver reported Intel UHD Graphics 730, OpenGL 4.6, GLSL 4.60, driver `32.0.101.7085`, compatibility profile mask `2`. The evidence label for the 25 matrix tests is **NATIVE GPU RENDER VERIFIED**. These are synthetic inputs to standalone variants. They are neither Minecraft screenshots nor a reconstruction of Iris/Oculus state, render order, lighting, texture atlases, or transparency composition. The tiny flat quad cannot validate world geometry, view-dependent continuity across a scene, or artistic correctness.

`scripts/tests/phase4-render-matrix.py` runs 25 matched V1.0.5/V1.0.6 cases: lit, dark, and torch terrain; sky and horizon; water and non-water translucent geometry; short underwater fog at the default distance, midpoint, and near endpoint; normal underwater fog; night vision; short air fog; clouds; rain; Nether; End; all five water profiles; night and horizon water; and Potato/Ultra underwater fog. A tiny quad extent makes vertex distance approximately 0.25 or 0.5 in the midpoint and endpoint cases. Control and treatment use the same installed driver, geometry, textures, uniforms, framebuffer, and state. The V1.0.5 `shaders/` tree was extracted from the frozen ZIP to ignored `artifacts/phase4-control-v105/`; its evaluated variants were generated with the Phase 2 `22dfaea` version of `generate-compiled-variants.ps1`, because the current generator's new Phase 3 Potato-cloud compile-out assertion intentionally rejects the historical control. The temporary historical generator was removed after generation. Both source manifests and generated variant hashes are checked at render time.

Per-case original float pixels, PNGs, result metadata, and three-panel control/treatment/4× RGB absolute-difference images are under `artifacts/phase4-gpu/compare-v105-v106/`. Alpha differences are recorded numerically. The aggregate comparison is `comparisons.json` (SHA-256 `9D4A4FA98CC11E50C0B577FFAA91A5A143F6ACB6513A34604DCB09B10EEBDCF7`); `assertions.json` is SHA-256 `3A3E665E000B3CE06B634712B6234548B430ABF96B2C2C03EABA1E4652228A3F`. ## Synthetic GPU timing

The Intel driver supports `GL_TIME_ELAPSED` timer queries. The renderer runs two warm-up queries and six measured queries per invocation, each around 2,000 identical quad draws; query time excludes CPU context creation, compile, texture setup, and readback. `phase4-gpu-timing.py` alternates control/treatment in `CTTCCTTC` order. This 32×32 workload is too small to infer Minecraft FPS. A difference below 2% is conservatively classified **NO MEASURABLE DIFFERENCE**.
