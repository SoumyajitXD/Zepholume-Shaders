## Final night-water decision

`fragment.glsl` first multiplies base water albedo by the sampled lightmap. `zephWaterSurfaceLinear` subsequently blends analytical reflected sky and adds celestial specular without a local sky-visibility input. Consequently reflected sky/highlights can remain even when base irradiance is zero. This is a **confirmed, unresolved source limitation**, not proof of the severity or cause of a particular gameplay screenshot.

`zephLightCoord.y` is available in the fragment, but its propagated ambient skylight is not a directional roof/terrain visibility test. The water function does not receive it. Passing that component and multiplying reflection by an arbitrary curve would be inexpensive but cannot reliably distinguish all open-sky, overhang, cave and nighttime cases. Multiplying by total sampled irradiance would additionally let block light influence sky reflection. The existing inputs provide no reliable local directional visibility information.

The release therefore retains the established reflection behavior, documents the limitation, and avoids a speculative global brightness or skylight multiplier. Open-sky reflectance and non-Overworld exclusions remain intact. No shadow map, tracing system or new pass is justified for this finalization.

## Performance and stability assessment

The interpolation repair removes one scalar varying, vertex length and vertex division, while introducing a fragment-local length for distance consumers. This is a correctness tradeoff, not a guaranteed speedup: fragment cost depends on coverage and driver optimization. The hand correction adds the required lightmap texture fetch. Removing that fetch would restore a confirmed lighting defect.

Camera-basis helpers introduce world-up dot products and bounded normalization; repeated uniform/direction expressions may be optimized by a compiler, but that optimization is not assumed. Existing compile-time quality gates, conditional normal calculation, Potato water/cloud feature elimination, water working-space processing and exact-zero celestial lobe gating remain. Water uses multiply chains for its fixed specular powers and adds no expensive postprocessing system. No measurable new benefit justified a last-minute rewrite.

Historical Phase 8 Intel flat-quad timer queries reported approximately **3.1% lower water time**, **2.44% higher hand time**, and **less than 0.16% A/A drift**. Those measurements predate this finalization and apply only to that synthetic fixture and driver. They are not Minecraft FPS, frame-pacing results, general GPU performance, or a comparison of all final V1.0.6 work against V1.0.5.

No shader algorithm was edited by this audit. Fresh final-source compilation, portability and focused numerical/GPU regressions are consolidated in the final release report. Actual fluid meshes, game transparency state, empty/opaque/translucent/emissive held items, night/cave visual balance, loader transformations, multi-vendor rendering and Minecraft performance remain unperformed runtime qualification. The user's explicit decision permits final artifact export with these limitations.
