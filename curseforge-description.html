01 / The experience

A familiar world, seen in a different light.

Zepholume connects sky, light, clouds and atmosphere while keeping Minecraft's blocks, colours and landscapes recognisable. V1.0.7 makes those effects more cohesive without adding a heavyweight rendering pipeline.

Light & colour

Sun and moon response, block-light warmth and controlled colour treatment give scenes a considered sense of depth. Local skylight exposure helps keep enclosed water and cave scenes grounded.

Sky & atmosphere

Sky, fog, layered block-shaped clouds and weather shape the mood as conditions change. V1.0.7 blends the Overworld sky more smoothly into horizon fog and corrects directional fog response. The Nether and End retain their own atmospheric paths.

Water & surroundings

Analytical water shading adds grazing-angle reflectance and surface response; Balanced and higher profiles also enable gentle water movement. V1.0.7 improves cave-water skylight gating and waterfall material interpolation. Effects vary by profile.

02 / Find your balance

Five profiles. One philosophy.

Start with Balanced, the source default and recommended starting point. Choose a lower tier to reduce optional work or a higher tier for more analytical detail.

Potato — The minimum-work tier retains the basic scene grade and loader sky while leaving major analytical effects and water waves off.

Low — Adds modest lighting, cloud, weather and water response, without water vertex movement.

Balanced • Recommended
Adds material response and bounded water movement for the intended everyday experience.

High — Raises selected lighting, cloud, water and weather quality.

Ultra — Uses the highest configured cloud, water and weather tiers within the same renderer.

All five profiles passed V1.0.7 configuration checks and local Iris profile switching was smoke-tested. Full visual testing across profiles, GPUs and loaders remains incomplete. Existing settings can override defaults, so select a profile explicitly when starting.

03 / Designed with restraint

Atmosphere without an oversized pipeline.

Zepholume uses a direct rendering path and adjusts analytical detail across its profiles. It does not add shadow maps, screen-space reflections or a bloom pipeline. The aim is to make a considered visual difference while keeping rendering work bounded.

That architecture is a design choice, not a promise of higher FPS. Performance depends on your GPU, resolution, render distance, loader, mods and scene.

Release notes / V1.0.7

Smoother horizons. Richer clouds. More grounded light.

V1.0.7 refines the relationship between sky, fog, clouds, surface lighting and water while retaining Zepholume's direct, single-colour-target architecture.

  • Sky and sunset: Blends the Overworld sky into loader horizon fog to remove the hard seam observed in older candidates.
  • Directional fog: Corrects the camera-to-scene ray orientation used in sunlight scattering.
  • Dark scenes: Preserves dark and black fog in Potato instead of unintentionally washing it brighter.
  • Layered clouds: Adds block-shaped coverage fields, weather-responsive openings and warm twilight facet lighting.
  • Cave water: Suppresses sun and moon highlights where local skylight exposure is absent.
  • Waterfalls and hands: Corrects water-material interpolation artifacts and retains lightmap illumination for hands and held items.

Testing status: The release record reports 320 standalone shader-stage compilation checks and 420 program-link checks passed. In a local Minecraft 26.1.2 / Fabric / Iris session on Intel UHD Graphics 730, the Ultra pipeline loaded without reported GLSL compiler or OpenGL errors in the inspected log; the horizon transition was compared visually against older candidates. Broad GPU, Oculus/Forge, extended gameplay and FPS qualification remain open. No FPS improvement is claimed.

04 / Get started

From download to daylight.

  1. Set up a Minecraft Java instance with an appropriate Iris shader loader for your game version.
  2. Download Zepholume from the CurseForge Files page.
  3. Place the intact ZIP in that instance's shaderpacks folder. Do not extract it.
  4. Open the shader-pack menu in Minecraft, select Zepholume and choose Balanced to start.

Compatibility: The final V1.0.7 build has local Iris runtime evidence on Minecraft 26.1.2 with Intel UHD Graphics 730. Oculus/Forge and cross-vendor gameplay testing remain limited; other combinations are not guaranteed. OptiFine is unsupported. CurseForge version tags describe file listings, not proof of gameplay testing for every version, loader or GPU.

05 / Need a hand?

Make the next step a clear one.

If performance is uncomfortable, try a lower profile and compare the same scene with the same settings. If the pack fails to load or looks wrong, check the instance, ZIP and loader versions, then try a minimal setup without unrelated rendering mods or resource packs.

For a useful report, include the Zepholume version and profile, Minecraft and loader versions, GPU and driver, reproduction steps, the complete latest.log, and a screenshot for visual issues.
