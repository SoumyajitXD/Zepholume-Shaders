01  /  The experience

More feeling in every familiar block.

Zepholume builds atmosphere through deliberate adjustments to the light, colour and environment already in Minecraft. The result aims to feel cohesive across exploration, building and survival—without swapping in an oversized effects pipeline.

01   Living atmosphere

Skies, horizons and fog respond together as daylight gives way to twilight, night and changing weather.

02   Grounded lighting

Directional sun and moon response, restrained ambient treatment and block-light warmth are designed to respect the scene.

03   Water with character

Profile-scaled water shading, subtle surface movement on Balanced and above, and restrained underwater atmosphere.

04   Controlled colour

Carefully bounded contrast, exposure, saturation and highlight treatment aim for depth without overwhelming Minecraft's identity.

03  /  Choose your balance

Five profiles. One philosophy.

Every profile works within Zepholume's direct-path architecture. Lower tiers leave optional work out; higher tiers refine the same approach instead of replacing it with a heavier renderer.

Recommended starting point  •  Default

BALANCED

The intended everyday experience: atmospheric colour, material response, enhanced depth and gentle two-wave water movement. Start here, then adjust for your hardware.

POTATO

The minimal-work tier for weaker hardware or troubleshooting. Optional analytical effects are compiled out.

LOW

Lightweight atmospheric, cloud, weather and water treatment without animated water movement.

HIGH

More nuanced ambient, cloud and water response for systems with greater graphics headroom.

ULTRA

Zepholume's fullest available analytical detail, still within the same deliberately restrained rendering design.

All five profiles are defined in the V1.0.6 shader configuration; Potato menu visibility still needs a qualifying in-game check. See the full profile guide.

04  /  The current release

V1.0.6 — Better light. Cleaner water.

V1.0.6 focuses on rendering correctness: water interpolation is more consistent across triangle boundaries, and hand shading now routes environmental lightmap information through the intended shader programs. The same lightweight renderer and five quality profiles remain.

  • Water shading: repaired normalized-ray and vertex-distance interpolation errors that produced triangle-diagonal discontinuities in GPU tests, without changing water waves or alpha settings.
  • Hand lighting: corrected missing lightmap configuration in four hand shader wrappers, allowing synthetic block-light and skylight inputs to affect hand illumination instead of remaining full-bright.
  • Atmosphere preserved: retains the previous lighting, fog and colour work without an unsupported global contrast or nighttime brightness retune.
  • Five defined profiles: Potato, Low, Balanced, High and Ultra remain available in the shader configuration, with Balanced as the recommended starting point.
  • Offline validation: Phase 9 reported 272 shader-stage checks, 420 link checks, 630 portability cases, 63 hand draws and 52 water draws passing.

Release and testing transparency

These are source-level and offline GPU results, not completed Minecraft visual qualification. The original waterfall and held-item scenes still require matched in-game comparison. Covered nighttime water may show overly bright analytical reflections, and no measured Minecraft FPS gain is claimed. V1.0.6 source is available in the repository; check the CurseForge Files page for the actual downloadable release version.

05  /  Performance by design

Less machinery. More Minecraft.

A visually ambitious pack does not need an endless chain of render passes. Zepholume uses a GLSL 330 direct-path design, one colour target and compile-time quality boundaries to keep the architecture understandable and the work bounded.

Deliberately not included

Shadow-map pipelines  •  Screen-space reflections  •  SSAO  •  Bloom pipelines  •  Temporal/TAA systems  •  Volumetric lighting  •  Ray tracing  •  Heavy post-processing chains

Efficient design is a goal, not an FPS guarantee. Actual results depend on GPU, drivers, resolution, settings and mod combinations; controlled V1.0.6 gameplay benchmarks remain outstanding. Read the architecture overview.

06  /  Get started

From download to daylight.

Installation

  1. Set up Minecraft Java Edition with a compatible Iris or Oculus shader loader.
  2. On the Files page, download Zepholume-Shaders-1.0.6.zip when it is listed; until then, use the latest actually available release.
  3. Place the ZIP in that instance's shaderpacks folder. Do not extract it.
  4. Choose Zepholume in the shader menu, then select Balanced.

Full installation guide

Compatibility

Zepholume targets Iris and Oculus-style shader environments. The established staging environments include Minecraft 1.20.1 with Iris/Fabric or Oculus/Forge; V1.0.6 has not completed full matched gameplay qualification on either.

A Minecraft 26.1.2 Fabric/Iris testing instance was prepared for V1.0.6, while other modern-version lanes remain static or experimental. Listing a version in file metadata does not establish runtime support. OptiFine is not a tested or supported target.
