01 / The experience

A familiar world, seen in a different light.

Zepholume connects sky, light, clouds and atmosphere while keeping Minecraft's blocks, colours and landscapes recognisable. V1.0.8 brings those effects closer together, refining horizon colour, twilight reflections and distant terrain while keeping the same restrained renderer.

Light & colour

Sun and moon response, warm block light and measured colour grading give scenes depth. V1.0.8 also refines distant terrain colour and restores hurt and flash overlays.

Sky & atmosphere

Sky, fog, layered block-shaped clouds and weather set the mood as conditions change. V1.0.8 coordinates horizon colour across the Overworld sky, terrain and clouds, with directional twilight haze and bounded rain response. The Nether and End retain their own atmospheric paths.

Water & surroundings

Analytical water shading adds grazing-angle reflectance and directional twilight colour. Local skylight exposure limits reflections in enclosed water, while Balanced and higher profiles enable gentle water movement. Reflections approximate the sky rather than mirror nearby geometry. Effects vary by profile.

02 / Find your balance

Five profiles. One philosophy.

Start with Balanced, the source default and recommended starting point. Choose a lower tier to reduce optional work or a higher tier for more analytical detail.

Potato — The minimum-work tier retains the basic scene grade and loader sky while leaving major analytical effects and water waves off.

Low — Adds modest lighting, cloud, weather and water response, without water vertex movement.

Balanced • Recommended
Adds material response and bounded water movement for the intended everyday experience.

High — Raises selected lighting, cloud, water and weather quality.

Ultra — Uses the highest configured cloud, water and weather tiers within the same renderer.

V1.0.8 retains the five-profile design and specializes selected rendering paths to avoid unnecessary work. Full in-game profile, GPU and loader testing for this update remains incomplete. Existing settings can override defaults, so select a profile explicitly when starting.

03 / Designed with restraint

Atmosphere without an oversized pipeline.

Zepholume uses a direct rendering path and adjusts analytical detail across its profiles. It does not add shadow maps, screen-space reflections or a bloom pipeline. The aim is to make a considered visual difference while keeping rendering work bounded.

V1.0.8 refines profile-specific shader work, particularly on Potato and Low. Controlled Intel UHD Graphics 730 synthetic tests recorded lower GPU execution times for selected shader workloads relative to an earlier V1.0.8 development build. These are not gameplay benchmarks or guaranteed FPS gains. Actual performance depends on your GPU, driver, resolution, render distance, loader, mods and scene.

Release notes / V1.0.8

One horizon. Calmer water. Less redundant work.

V1.0.8 builds on the previous release's horizon and lightmap corrections, with a more consistent atmospheric palette and targeted shader optimizations.

  • Sky, fog and clouds: Coordinates horizon colours across rendering paths and refines directional cloud haze.
  • Water: Refines twilight reflections and reduces excessive analytical reflection work where skylight exposure is absent.
  • Terrain: Improves distant colour neutrality to reduce unwanted tint shifts.
  • Entity overlays: Restores hurt and flash colour response.
  • Animation: Addresses phase discontinuities in foliage and water movement.
  • Shader efficiency: Specializes selected paths across Potato, Low and higher profiles while retaining the single-colour-target architecture.

Testing status: The V1.0.8 development report records standalone compilation and program-link checks, offline loader transformations, portability regressions and reproducible packaging. These are development results, not proof of comprehensive Minecraft runtime support. 04 / Get started

From download to daylight.

  1. Set up a Minecraft Java instance with an appropriate Iris loader, or a compatible Oculus setup where available for your game version.
  2. Download the available Zepholume ZIP for your setup from the CurseForge Files page.
  3. Place the intact ZIP in that instance's shaderpacks folder. Do not extract it.
  4. Open the shader-pack menu in Minecraft, select Zepholume and choose Balanced to start.

Compatibility: Zepholume targets Minecraft Java 1.20 through 26.3 with Iris and compatible Oculus configurations. Earlier V1.0.7 local runtime evidence on Minecraft 26.1.2 / Iris / Intel UHD Graphics 730 does not qualify V1.0.8. Oculus/Forge and cross-vendor gameplay testing remain limited. OptiFine is unsupported. Check exact loader and Minecraft version requirements; file tags do not establish runtime verification.

05 / Need a hand?

Make the next step a clear one.

If performance is uncomfortable, try a lower profile and compare the same scene with the same settings. If the pack fails to load or looks wrong, check the instance, ZIP and loader versions, then try a minimal setup without unrelated rendering mods or resource packs.

For a useful report, include the Zepholume version and profile, Minecraft and loader versions, GPU and driver, reproduction steps, the complete latest.log, and a screenshot for visual issues.
